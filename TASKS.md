  (a) **NSE spells its own title date four ways** (`Jul 28, 2026` · `Mar 03,2021` · `July 02, 2018` · `Mar 20 2020`). A strict format string parsed 2026 correctly and silently rejected **half of recorded history**. Month/day/year are now resolved separately via `nse.parse_month_name`, which also removes the `%b` locale dependency `nse.py` had already flagged.
  (b) **NSE's own participant files do not always reconcile.** Across 48 files sampled 2015→2026, 11 carry a discrepancy between the category rows and the published `TOTAL`; the worst is **exactly 1 contract**. An exact-equality check — the obvious design — would have halted the feed on the most recent real trading day. Tolerance is 4 (one per category), against columns running to 16 million.
  (c) **The `TOTAL` cross-check is weaker than it looks, and the docstring says so.** A *uniform* column reorder shifts the `TOTAL` row identically, so the sums still agree. What actually guards against drift is parsing by header name; the reconciliation earns its place by catching a **changed participant taxonomy** (a fifth category would silently vanish from every sum) and truncated rows.
  (d) **⚠️ Wiring requirement for whoever assembles these sources (1.1's ingestion agent / `backfill.py`): pass ONE shared `RateLimiter` to all four NSE sources.** `nse.py`, `delivery.py`, `participants.py` and `fnoban.py` all hit `nsearchives.nseindia.com`, and each *defaults* to its own 2 req/s courtesy limiter — so a naive wiring issues up to **8 req/s at one free host**, and being blocked by NSE is a halted data plane (invariant #10). Every constructor already accepts an injected limiter, so this is a wiring fix, not a code change. Deliberately *not* solved by a module-level shared default: that would make every unit test wait on a real 2/s limiter (each test currently builds a fresh source, and a fresh limiter never sleeps on first acquire).
  (e) **The ban list is a compliance boundary, so its API shape is deliberate.** It exposes only a set and a boolean — no "days in ban" counter, no ban-frequency series. A numeric feature here is something the learning loop could learn to *seek* rather than respect (invariant #4). `fno_entry_blocked` is named for its exact scope: F&O only (cash delivery is unaffected) and entry only (exits are always permitted — a veto that trapped us in a position would be worse than the risk the ban controls). A missing file raises rather than returning an empty set: "we cannot confirm what is banned" and "nothing is banned" must never be the same answer.

    (d) **The look-ahead sweep was weaker than it looked, and the fix was measured, not assumed.** Reintroducing the confirmation-lag bug (placing a swing on the bar it occurred rather than the bar it became knowable) and running the 1.4a sweep caught **6 of 33** affected words.     (f) **Hand fixtures with `open == close` silently disabled a third of the coverage.** The 1.4a builder set open equal to close, so every *body*-dependent word (order blocks, mitigation, `imbalance_ratio`) computed on zero-height candles and returned all-`nan` — which passes the look-ahead sweep trivially while appearing covered. A breaker now dies on the mirror of the rule that created it, and is tested *before* the block on each bar — gating it on "no live block" kept the dead breaker alive precisely when it died, because the impulse that takes a breaker out is usually the one that forms the next block.

A ranking cannot be computed that way: knowing RELIANCE is 7th-best today requires every other symbol's bar *for today*.     (d) 🔴 **`rsi(14) < 30` was not expressible — and had not been since 1.4a.** `above`/`below` take two *series*, which is the right composable shape, but nothing could stand on the other side of the comparison: there was no way to say "30".   (d) **The purge gap is derived from the strategy, not configured.** A 252-day word leaks 252 days across each walk-forward seam; a 20-day word leaks 20. **The three strategies scored** (in-sample → walk-forward OOS → cost/tax stress → DSR/PBO → replay → live sim):

| # | Strategy | Role | Measured trades/yr | Sim eligibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SMA(20/50) cross | **Engine control.** Proves the machinery on something trivial; expected to have no edge. | 18 | ❌ **Backtest only** — barred from live sim and canary (20 months to a verdict) |
| 2 | Donlevey liquidity-sweep reversal | The operator's chosen direction, distilled to daily bars | 84 | ✅ |

**On strategy 3, stated plainly:** Sharpe 2.9 on a long-biased equity strategy is roughly triple what the best systematic equity funds sustain, from free data in the most-analysed 50 stocks in India. The four ordinary explanations — survivorship (using *today's* Nifty-50 list across 2012–2025), a 5× bull market, an optimistic cost model, and a ±0.6 standard error on Sharpe — are each far more likely than a genuine discovery. **We reproduce it inside our own gate or we do not cite it.** If our survivorship-free reproduction with real costs returns Sharpe 0.6, that result is *more valuable than a passing strategy*: it proves the engine catches a number a published paper got wrong. Either outcome is a win; believing it untested is the only losing move.

**The gate** (all must pass; thresholds in `goal.yaml`):

- Sharpe **lower confidence bound > 0** at 95%, net of cost **and** tax — never the point estimate
- OOS Sharpe ≥ 0.6 × IS Sharpe (`oos_decay_max: 0.40`)
- **DSR ≥ 0.95** on effective trials **including every hand-authored attempt** · **PBO ≤ 0.50**
- ≥ 100 OOS trades to promote · 30–99 → `NEEDS_MORE_DATA` · < 30 → `REJECTED`
- ≥ 3 years OOS spanning a benchmark drawdown ≥ 15% *(verified present: Sep-24→Mar-25 at 15.8%, Jan-26→Mar-26 at 15.2%)*
- After-cost alpha > 0 vs Nifty · benchmark R² < 0.80 — otherwise it is leveraged beta
- Survives 1.5× cost stress, the **crypto VDA** tax stress **and** the **equity business-income** tax stress
- Max drawdown ≤ 8% — tied to the −10% live kill-switch, which a 25%-drawdown strategy would trip immediately
- **Replay reproduces backtest signals bar-for-bar**; live-mode sim has ≥3 weeks and full event coverage
