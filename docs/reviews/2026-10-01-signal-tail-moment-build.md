## Mathematics and numerical behavior
Original weights are n_g/N. Original all-trade mean is sum(w_g*y_g), not a mean of group means with equal weights and not a weighted average of declared centers. Direct paired-excess uses every raw-minus-benchmark pair and its own group moment premise; no component independence or moment subtraction is assumed.

Exact V=sum(w_g^2*M_g) bounds the original weighted-mean variance under whole-group independence. The upper radius encloses sqrt(V/alpha). Centered moment bounds may differ from actual variance; no true expectation is provided to the estimator. Chebyshev gives unconditional original-mean coverage under the declared law. The derivation and primary references are in the [approved proposal](../plans/2026-10-01-signal-tail-moment-proposal.md).

Fractions retain exact numeric inputs, weights, means and V. A fresh100-digit Decimal context encloses the radius; its represented square is certified against exact V/alpha, with an outward step or refusal when needed. Decimal.sqrt's nearest rounding is not mistaken for a directed certificate. Endpoints are rounded outward from exact mean +/- represented radius. Endpoint precision is sized from bounded exact operands, keeping huge legitimate outcomes and tiny positive uncertainty from collapsing. Resource guards inspect Decimal coefficient/exponent and integer/Fraction bit sizes before expensive conversion; technical limits are not market floors.

The predeclared precision request is absolute mathematical FULL width2h, in original metric units. Too-wide results carry diagnostics and no endpoints. Width does not depend on observed variance/range or favorable outcomes. full_width encloses2*radius; outward rounded endpoint span can be slightly larger. Exact Fraction mean remains available, including nonterminating rational zero-moment centers. No conditional-on-emission coverage promise or endpoint clipping.
