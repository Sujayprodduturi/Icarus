## What changed and why
The simulator can keep every possible completed trade while the separate research calculator judges how uncertain its original average is. This extension accounts together for rare large outcomes and shared/persistent market influence under an explicitly declared artificial law. It keeps original trade counts and paired benchmark differences; no trimming or invented independent groups.

Implemented in scripts/research/signal_moment_uncertainty.py, with GaussianMomentModelContract, estimate_persistent and PersistentMomentResult. The existing bounded module only extracts stationary/local premises and latent-map validation. Original independent-moment and bounded/persistent APIs retain type/validation ordering and numerical behavior. No new dependency, external I/O or product integration.

The model declares a stationary Gaussian AR factor with known absolute persistence below1, complete distinct actual coordinates, fixed whole-group raw/benchmark local maps and mutually independent local noise independent of the entire factor process. Whole-group centered second moments are trusted actual-law bounds; neither observed moments nor sample correlation certify them. Returns themselves need not be Gaussian or bounded. Unknown premises refuse.
