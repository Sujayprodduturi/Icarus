## Required changes

1. **P1 — Make the first slice runnable end to end.** `timed_runner.py:871–905` unconditionally constructs the strict benchmark panel and refuses without the exact close. Adding a candle matcher cannot remove that earlier refusal. Specify a narrow opt-in route through loader, panel, timed evaluation and report, with a test that reaches an actual synthetic trade using candle-only benchmark records. Preserve the exact default. Explicitly refuse benchmark-dependent signal cards unless their daily inputs have the separate modeled availability treatment; do not synthesize strict observations. Reuse `TimedTrade`, `Timing`, existing source seals and cost allocations. Avoid a second runner or speculative general schema framework.
2. **P1 — Separate decision causality from retrospective measurement.** An entry candle finishing after an entry cannot inform that decision, but its completed range can describe the historical interval retrospectively. Do not impose the exact matcher's `available_ts <= fill_ts` on this retrospective comparison. Signal and management inputs must obey modeled availability and next-bar rules. Interval-end/next-session-open availability is an explicit replay assumption, not a proven conservative bound on original publication. Unknown revision/publication facts must remain unknown; the model does not create point-in-time certification.
3. **P1 — Specify comparable cash flows and the denominator.** For the current single-entry `TimedTrade`, define stock return as fully allocated realised trading P&L divided by `entry.price * original_quantity`, using executed quantity, never intended quantity, R-multiple or account equity. Include all entry/exit costs once. Require complete exits and matching adjustment basis. The benchmark weights describe liquidating fractions of a fixed initial benchmark holding at the same exit schedule; they are not a compounded strategy/portfolio return. Multi-entry, open-position and reinvested portfolio comparison remain unsupported in this first slice. Do not introduce an aggregate return until its cash-flow rule is reviewed.
4. **P1 — Positive prices, signed returns.** Replace “Require positive finite bounds” with positive finite OHLC price inputs, ordered OHLC envelopes and finite ordered return endpoints. Negative and zero return endpoints are valid. Independently calculated fixture: entry captured range `[100,110]`, two equal exits `[120,130]` and `[90,100]` yield `[-1/22,3/20]`. A stock entered at 100 and equally exited at 125/95, with total costs of 2 per original share, returns `2/25`; its descriptive excess interval is `[-7/100,69/550]`. This fixture detects quantity and fee normalization defects as well as rejection of negative returns.
5. **P2 — Correct the requirement attribution.** PRD §31.5 requires positive alpha after cost/tax and benchmark-relative metrics; it does not explicitly say an “investable” benchmark with simulated index brokerage/tax is mandatory. Remove that asserted PRD requirement. Net-trading-cost stock return beside gross Price Index return is a legitimate explicitly asymmetric descriptive convention. Neither that convention nor an interval subtraction establishes regression alpha or after-tax promotion evidence. Preserve `promotion_eligible=false` and keep tax settlement separate.
6. **P2 — Do not let this proxy become an endless extra gate.** Approve one synthetic vertical slice with the above arithmetic, causal refusal, missing/overlap/coverage, strict-default regression and counted-run predecode protections. Its stopping point is a working descriptive candle path, followed by qualification of a bounded actual-data window and the existing actual-run authorization. Full source qualification is independently necessary; exact tick publication histories must not become a prerequisite of a mode expressly modeling availability, unless a specific PIT claim actually needs them. Missing universe members must remain explicit; choosing only surviving retrievable securities is unacceptable.

## Source-readiness interpretation

The initial source audit's cited terms were silent about several retention details. New primary evidence supersedes treating this as mere silence: independently opened [Kite staff clarification, 9 October 2026, section 5](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/16304/written-clarification-for-self-built-kite-connect-trading-system) permits personal analysis but states that permanent storage is restricted; it separately restricts paper trading with live data. That is a substantive restriction requiring a disposition for the intended reproducible archive. It supplies no retention duration or complete historical-backtest retention rule, so neither an arbitrary expiry nor blanket prohibition on private historical analysis follows. Resolve the applicable subscription/contract and exact archive use; do not invent a rights waiver or demand separate express wording for every backup when an applicable provision resolves it. This review makes no legal conclusion and does not lift the existing hold. Other sections of that forum answer do not amend this project's order safety rules.

Captured high/low scenarios are not guaranteed market-price bounds when ticks can be missing. The proposal correctly identifies this limitation; retain it in every comparison output. Likewise missing publisher timestamps cannot be replaced by retrieval timestamps. Calendar coverage, dated identity/universe completeness and common action basis remain concrete qualification work, independent of whether this arithmetic is approved.
