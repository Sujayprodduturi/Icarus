## What is established, and what is not

**Established:** the affected opening discrepancies are not explained by a missing M15 grid row: every selected M15 day has its full expected 25 timestamps, and its first observed open equals the Kite DAY open. Those coincident Kite values differ from contemporaneous NSE raw for INFY, HDFCBANK, and RELIANCE only. The raw NSE cache was independently shown faithful for all 597 names and all six fields on these dates. This establishes a cross-source, retrieval-time incompatibility; retrospective rebasing is the leading explanation, not a certified cause.

Kite staff state that historical data is retrospectively adjusted for bonuses, splits, rights, spin-offs, and extraordinary dividends, with volume adjusted for bonus/split/rights/demerger but not dividends; a second staff answer says demergers wait for the applicable COA file and recommends comparison with exchange bhavcopy ([corporate-action answer](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/15971/getting-the-right-closing-prices-adjusted-for-corporate-actions), [split/demerger answer](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/16099/split-adjustment-data)). A 2024 staff answer says historical dividend computations follow the exchange circular and the dividend is subtracted, but does not specify a historical-series factor, reference value, or rounding algorithm ([dividend answer](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/14322/is-historical-data-adjusted-to-dividends-if-not-how-to-calculate-total-returns-need-latest-update)). Those statements establish a mutable adjustment mechanism, but they do not publish this packet's complete per-security transforms or a dedicated corporate-action API.

Event evidence ranks the hypotheses as follows:

1. **HDFCBANK — strongly event-consistent, exact vendor implementation still unpinned.** HDFC Bank allotted a 1:1 bonus with record date 2025-08-27 ([issuer filing mirrored by SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1144967/000119312525191696/d22509dex99.htm)). The five pre-event rows show exact `2/1` DAY/NSE volume and price ratios at or near `1/2`; deviations are consistent with quantization, but the rounding rule is unproved. That is the event class and direction described by Kite staff. It does not by itself prove the vendor's exact factor lineage for these bytes.
2. **RELIANCE — independently event-derived candidate factor; provider implementation unproved.** The issuer's COA filing gives RIL 95.32% retained tax-cost allocation and RSIL/JFSL 4.68%, with one RSIL share per RIL share ([issuer COA filing](https://www.ril.com/sites/default/files/2023-08/SEIntimation_ApportionmentofCost_0.pdf)). Reliance then announced a 1:1 bonus in 2024 ([issuer filing](https://www.ril.com/sites/default/files/2024-09/SE_05092024_0.pdf)). If Kite uses the retained COA percentage as its demerger price multiplier, as its statement that it waits for the COA file suggests, the independently event-derived cumulative candidate is exactly `(9532/10000)/2 = 2383/5000`. The five captured price ratios are consistent with that candidate under tick quantization. Their volume ratios are consistent with its inverse `5000/2383`, but Kite does not publish the price-multiplier, inverse-volume, or integer-rounding formula. Neither candidate came from observed prices; neither is yet a certified vendor transform.
3. **INFY — identified event chain; historical-series factor missing.** Infosys reported a 2024 final dividend of Rs 20 plus a special dividend of Rs 8, record date 2024-05-31 ([issuer filing in NSE archive](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/INFY_18042024165501_outcome.pdf)). NSE then issued a 2026 adjustment circular for an INFY dividend of Rs 25 effective 2026-06-10 ([NSE notice](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FAOP73942.pdf)); its follow-up reduces each listed option strike by exactly Rs 25 ([NSE revision](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FAOP74629.pdf)). NSE's published derivatives procedure classifies dividends at or above 2% of the prescribed market value as extraordinary and deducts the full dividend from strikes ([NSE procedure](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-derivatives-corporate-actions-adjustments)). These action terms and Kite's stated no-volume adjustment for dividends supply a concrete event chain consistent with the observed class and direction without fitting the ratio. They do not prove the chain is complete or caused the snapshot shift, nor specify how Kite converts cash adjustments into a retrospective OHLC factor, which reference value it uses, or its candle rounding. INFY is therefore `FAIL_EVENT_FACTOR_MISSING`, not source-certified. TCS remains the `1/1` negative control against a universal scale.
4. **Residual alternatives:** a security-identity error is disfavoured by the bound retrieval token, unaffected controls, and security-specific event-shaped signatures. It remains falsifiable where current provider identity and official ISIN disagree or where dated NSE lineage records a security change. An old per-day provider token is not required for an unchanged cash equity. A pure units error is likewise disfavoured but remains testable through the identity and event ledger.

## Minimal repair specification

1. Preserve contemporaneous official NSE daily rows as point-in-time eligibility and audit authority. Keep every series explicitly basis-tagged. PRD §29.3's split/bonus/rights adjustment and fixed-price-return rule must be implemented from independent events; never rewrite the raw evidence or infer a factor from Kite/NSE ratios.
2. Bind the retrieval-day Kite master/token to the current official NSE ISIN, then follow dated NSE symbol/series/ISIN and event lineage across actual changes. Do not impose unavailable per-day historical provider-token records as a universal gate for unchanged cash equities. Preserve the exact request and response hashes used for every cell.
3. Add an independently sourced corporate-action ledger containing event type, ex/record dates, event terms, price factor, quantity factor, source URL/hash, and as-of time. Derive transforms from the event terms and documented provider convention; never fit a factor to observed prices.
4. Keep simulated fills, costs, and integer historical share/lot quantities on the contemporaneous tradable basis. Do not combine retrospectively adjusted half-prices with ordinary one-share lots: that manufactures fractional original shares and changes costs. A restored M15 path must transform both price and quantity consistently or use a contemporaneous raw provider series.
5. Reconcile Kite DAY and M15 separately. Use only a documented deterministic tick-rounding rule. Where inverse tick rounding maps an adjusted observation to more than one possible raw value, retain the exact candidate interval and fail `QUANTIZATION_AMBIGUITY`; do not widen an equality tolerance or choose a fitted per-day factor. Compare opens/extrema/volume only after both sides have a proven common basis; do not require universal DAY-open equality or last-M15-close equality.
6. Emit one exact residual class per cell: `PASS_RAW_EQUAL`, `PASS_EVENT_RECONCILED`, `FAIL_IDENTITY_LINEAGE`, `FAIL_EVENT_FACTOR_MISSING`, `FAIL_ROUNDING_RULE_UNKNOWN`, `FAIL_QUANTIZATION_AMBIGUITY`, `FAIL_INTRADAY_CAPTURE_GAP`, or `FAIL_UNEXPLAINED_BASIS`. Use `FAIL_INTRADAY_CAPTURE_GAP` only with independent evidence of a capture gap; otherwise use `FAIL_UNEXPLAINED_BASIS`. Any failure remains a refusal.
7. Re-run the frozen five-day gate and a separate event-date fixture, then run the required full interval and warm-up qualification before readiness. Until INFY receives a provider-specific historical factor rule and RELIANCE receives a proved volume/rounding convention, real-data readiness remains blocked.
