# Time-resolved benchmark comparison — approved design

**Status: operator-approved design on 2026-09-26; authorises Step 6 planning only, not implementation or real-data evaluation.**
**Decision owner:** operator. **Design direction and written design approved:** 2026-09-26.

## 1. Purpose and boundary

Icarus must be able to say whether a modelled stock trade beat the official Nifty 50 **price index over the same entry-to-exit window**, and later learn separately from entry, exit, and order-execution timing. A daily session label is not an execution timestamp. The signal-test output remains diagnostic: return after modelled charges, before capital-gains tax. It is not a portfolio equity curve or the after-tax alpha required for strategy promotion.

This specification covers the time and data contract for that comparison. Step 6's statistical estimator/calibration needs its own specification and approval. Historical-provider procurement, a live broker adapter, automatic strategy changes, and any real-data signal evaluation are outside this specification. The existing lockbox and trial-accounting rules remain unchanged.

## 2. Why the current data cannot answer the question

- `FillModel` assigns `bar.ts` to every simulated fill. Current equity bars are daily sessions stamped at the normal open, even when a stop or target could have filled later in the day. The timestamp is a **bar identifier**, not a measured execution instant.
- The current Nifty series is daily. Its builder can forward-fill missing sessions, discards original source times, and caches prices without an observed-versus-filled provenance flag. A matching date does not establish a simultaneous index observation.
- `SignalExitFragment.benchmark_return` can currently be supplied as a Decimal without evidence of its source or sampling rule. The simulator correctly leaves it absent.
- Same-day partial delivery sells are charged a DP fee per sell call, while `goal.yaml` and the PRD specify one DP fee per symbol per day. A stale residual mark may also accrue a fictional sell-side fee. This pre-existing accounting discrepancy must be resolved or explicitly labelled before a cost-adjusted excess return is called contract-note exact.

## 3. Alternatives considered

1. **Daily-price approximation:** cheap and broad historical coverage, but exit-time selection is arbitrary for intraday stops/targets. It cannot be the official benchmark-relative signal result or an input to timing improvement.
2. **Time-resolved stock and Nifty observations (selected target):** replay stock execution at a declared temporal resolution, then compare to a causally available index observation. This costs more data and engineering work; missing history stays missing.
3. **Daily high/low excess-return bounds:** useful as a separate sensitivity check if provenance is repaired, but not a point estimate of alpha and not a substitute for option 2.

The operator selected option 2 as the long-run path, within the existing D3/D4/D7 boundaries: daily point-in-time universe and eligibility remain authoritative over 2011–2022, and the first intraday timing overlay is M15 over its separately pinned 2015–2022 lane. A name without an intraday record remains in the daily universe and gets an explicit missing-refinement result; it is never silently dropped. Option 1 may exist only as a visibly separate exploratory sensitivity measure after a separately reviewed sampling rule; this approval does not enable it.

## 4. Component and data contracts

### 4.1 Acquisition and isolation

The hosted Zerodha Kite MCP available to Codex is a **separate authenticated operator-side broker connection**, not an Icarus runtime dependency or data archive. Its advertised and exposed mutation capabilities disagree (the current tool inventory names order and GTT mutations), so treat it as **potentially write-capable** until effective server-side restrictions are verified; do not call mutation tools. It must not be imported by research, validation, or strategy agents. No broker credential or direct broker egress enters the research/learning plane. A paid Kite Connect app may be useful to assess available market history, but the ₹500 Connect plan also has order/account API capabilities; a data call does not make its account credential read-only. Source selection requires a separate security review of credential capability and one-way data delivery. A licensed non-broker data source is an acceptable alternative.

An approved acquisition boundary publishes immutable, versioned market-data artifacts to a read-only research store. A broker-sourced collector, if used, runs outside the research plane under a separate OS identity with no registered order-egress IP and an enforced market-data endpoint/method allowlist; merely promising not to call order methods is insufficient. The research plane reads artifacts, never calls the broker. A one-way artifact transfer and licence for internal archival/reuse require verification. Each artifact records canonical instrument, venue, vendor, source record ID, source revision, retrieval time, parser/schema version, raw content hash, covered session/event span, adjustment basis, and observed-versus-synthesised status. Absence, duplicate/conflicting observations, revisions, and coverage gaps are explicit. No raw credentials, request tokens, or access tokens enter artifacts or logs.

Provider selection must check the existing two-lane contract: daily point-in-time universe and eligibility (including delisted names) over 2011–2022, plus M15 equity/Nifty timing observations where available within the separately pinned 2015–2022 intraday lane. Do not quietly redefine the daily cohort to the years or instruments a convenient intraday provider has. Missing refinement is explicit and does not remove a delisted or otherwise eligible name from the daily cohort. Coverage and survivorship limits must be measured before any intraday performance claim; the intended analysis may remain unavailable if too little aligned history exists. A changed window or resolution needs a new explicit operator decision and pre-registered study, with a separate trial once real data is inspected. The 2023-onward lockbox is not used for provider comparison or calibration.

### 4.2 Event time and execution provenance

Every decision, order intent, simulated fill, partial fill, and exit/mark carries UTC-aware event time, original session identity, and a temporal-precision label: `SESSION_ONLY`, `BAR_RESOLVED`, or `EVENT_RESOLVED`. A bar-resolved record also carries bar start/end and whether event order inside it is known. A stop inferred only from an M15 candle's low has an **ambiguous interval**, not a single exact fill instant; stamping the bar open or close cannot turn it into one. Preserve source and recognition times separately for stale positions. Convert to IST only to validate the exchange calendar and actual regular/special-session boundaries.

The initial timing overlay uses **15-minute bars (M15)** under D4 and keeps the **strategy clock daily**: indicator lookbacks, decision bars, signal IDs, and time-stop holding periods remain in trading sessions. A daily signal is available only after its decision bar closes; its first eligible execution event is in the next session. Finer stock observations refine only the **execution clock** and do not turn a 20-session rule into 20 minutes. Intraday signal generation is a separate, versioned strategy hypothesis. The simulator must replay entry and exit mechanics against stock observations with a defined causal order for triggers, marketable limits, resting limits, partial fills, and no-fills. It may not reuse a daily high/low to assert an exact intraday sequence. Modelled queue/slippage assumptions stay explicit. Both signal and portfolio modes use the same shared execution semantics before their results are compared. The existing daily mode remains intact until a new mode passes its own frozen-characterisation and failure-path tests.

### 4.3 Benchmark observation and matching

The benchmark is the configured, versioned **Nifty 50 price index**, not a later constituent reconstruction, a Nifty futures price, or an unlabeled total-return index. Each index observation carries its actual event timestamp, availability timestamp, positive value, observed/stale state, and artifact identity. A benchmark leg can use only an observation available at or before a **resolved** modelled stock fill time, within a maximum age and price-selection rule that are dated and fixed **before** any real-data evaluation. If the stock fill is known only to an intrabar interval and Nifty moved within that interval, no official point estimate is emitted: return an ambiguity refusal or separately labelled bounds. This may be common with the approved first M15 overlay; 1-minute or event-level data would be a separately approved refinement, not a silent shortcut. A bar-start or bar-end convention may support an exploratory model-dependent estimate after separate review, never an exact-time claim. No future-nearest selection, interpolation, silent forward-fill, or use of a cached daily open as an intraday exit price is allowed.

For each genuinely filled exit fragment `j`, compute `B(exit_j) / B(entry) - 1` from valid observed index levels. Weight fragment returns by its fraction of the stock trade's original filled quantity (equivalently deployed entry capital under the current single-entry-price contract). One absent or invalid entry/exit leg makes the **whole trade** benchmark return and excess return unavailable; never reweight the observed fragments or replace a missing return with zero. Report missingness by reason and covered-trade count.

A `STALE_MARK` is a marked residual, not a filled sale. Its stock price-source time and recognition time differ; neither can be silently paired to an index level as an exact realised exit. Any trade containing such a residual has no exact whole-trade benchmark excess return. A separately labelled mark-to-market comparison would require a different contract.

The diagnostic measure is `trade.net_return - matched_nifty_price_return`, described as **pre-tax, net-of-modelled-strategy-charges benchmark excess return**. It is not regression alpha, an investable index-ETF return, or after-tax promotion alpha. Strategy charges and the benchmark price index are different economic objects; both components must be shown so readers can see that distinction. Within one signal trade, same-symbol/same-day executed sell fragments accrue one DP charge, not one per fragment. Separate hypothetical signal trades on the same symbol/day retain independent per-trade charge assumptions because signal mode has no shared account; the report must not call that total a contract-note-exact daily bill. Portfolio mode aggregates the actual account's executed sells once per symbol/day. A non-executed `STALE_MARK` is not a demat sale; any liquidation-fee estimate is separate and labelled. Correct or explicitly label these boundaries before calling the strategy-cost component exact. Preserve the 2026 cost-schedule-as-live-deployability convention rather than implying historical tax/cost rates.

## 5. Refusal and validation contract

Refuse a matched leg on missing execution time, unresolved intrabar order/time, session-only precision for a within-session fill, unknown/mismatched instrument or price basis, missing/stale/synthesised index observation, future observation, age beyond the registered limit, duplicate/conflicting event, nonpositive value, invalid timezone or calendar, or unverified source artifact. Refusal is typed and counted. It does not suppress valid raw trade outcomes or invent a benchmark zero.

Tests must cover: a daily stop falsely stamped at the open; an M15-bar stop with a sharp within-bar Nifty move that refuses a point estimate; daily strategy lookbacks and 20-session time stop unchanged under M15 execution replay; genuine open fill versus later within-bar fill; special-session times; future and stale index readings; missing/forward-filled benchmark sessions; duplicate and corrected source observations; one missing leg among several partial exits; quantity-weighted hand calculation; stale mark versus recognition; source hash/identity across cache round-trips; no-fill outcomes; UTC/IST boundaries; and preservation of the current portfolio's frozen daily trace. DP-fee red/green money-path tests cover same-day fragments within one signal trade, two separate same-day hypothetical signal trades, actual portfolio same-symbol/day sells, and a non-executed stale mark.

Before any real-data run, verify data coverage and artifact hashes without evaluating a strategy, freeze the matching rule, complete synthetic replay and missingness tests, and require the Step 7 trial-ledger barrier. A real full-development signal run and each inspected fold-matched run remain separate counted trials. An unavailable benchmark never becomes a favourable verdict. Statistical intervals and promotion remain disabled until their separate gates pass.

## 6. Delivery sequence and review gates

This is an umbrella architecture, not a new task queue. **Task 3a Step 6 statistical design remains next** and must integrate the sampling, cost, matching-floor, and refusal contracts from this approved design before its own plan is approved. Subsequent work may be scheduled only through the existing task order or an explicit operator-approved task amendment. Acquisition, execution replay, and cost-accounting changes each need scoped design/plan review when their data and assumptions are known. No implementation plan may enable benchmark point estimates before the source-specific maximum age and sampling rule are dated and approved.
